# URN Namespace Registration for Mplify Alliance

## Registration Request per RFC 8141, Appendix A

```
Namespace Identifier:

   mplify

Version:

   1

Date:

   2026-04-20

Registrant:

   Registering organization:

      Name:     Mplify Alliance (formerly MEF Forum)
      Address:  12130 Millennium Dr, Suite 300
                Los Angeles, CA 90094
                United States

   Designated contact:

      Role:    Manager, Mplify Namespace
      Email:   namespace@mplify.net

Purpose:

   Mplify Alliance (https://www.mplify.net/), formerly MEF Forum, is
   a global alliance of enterprises, service providers, cloud
   providers, data center operators, technology providers, and systems
   integrators, working together to accelerate the AI-powered digital
   economy through standardization, automation, certification, and
   collaboration.

   Mplify is the defining body for Carrier Ethernet, Lifecycle
   Service Orchestration (LSO) APIs, and certified SASE and SD-WAN.
   Mplify has developed the global blueprint for Network-as-a-Service
   (NaaS) that is empowering the industry to innovate, interoperate,
   and scale trusted network services across a global ecosystem.

   In June 2025, MEF Forum became Mplify Alliance.  The "mplify" URN
   namespace is being registered to provide persistent, unique
   identifiers for resources published under the Mplify Alliance
   brand, complementing the existing "mef" URN namespace registered
   in RFC 7818 [RFC7818].

   The "mplify" namespace will be used to identify resources such as:

      -  YANG modules and data models
      -  LSO (Lifecycle Service Orchestration) API specifications
      -  Product schemas and product specification identifiers
      -  Certification programs and artifacts
      -  Service definitions and standards
      -  Any other resources that require managed, persistent
         identification as part of Mplify Alliance activities

   The existing "mef" URN namespace (RFC 7818) remains valid.  URNs
   previously assigned under "urn:mef:*" continue to be valid and
   will not be reassigned or retired.  New resources will be assigned
   URNs under the "mplify" namespace.

Syntax:

   The syntax of namespace-specific strings for the "mplify"
   namespace is <NSS> in [RFC8141].

Assignment:

   Assignment of URNs within the "mplify" namespace is managed by
   Mplify Alliance through the Mplify Assigned Names and Numbers
   (MANN) registry (https://www.mplify.net/MANN/).

   Assignment is made by Mplify Alliance in accordance with the
   policies and procedures it defines for each class of resource.
   Mplify Alliance may delegate assignment authority for specific
   resource classes to recognized working groups, committees, or
   partner organizations, but retains ultimate authority over the
   namespace.

   URNs are assigned as part of the publication of Mplify Alliance
   specifications, standards, data models, or certification programs.
   Each assigned URN is documented in the MANN registry.

   Mplify Alliance guarantees the uniqueness of all URN assignments
   within the "mplify" namespace.

Security and Privacy:

   There are no additional security considerations other than those
   normally associated with the use and resolution of URNs in
   general, which are described in "Uniform Resource Names (URNs)"
   [RFC8141].

   URNs in the "mplify" namespace identify publicly available
   specifications, data models, and related resources.  These URNs
   do not themselves contain or expose private or personally
   identifiable information.

   Since the "mplify" namespace assigns URNs to publicly available
   resources, directory harvesting is not a significant concern.

   Mplify Alliance does not assign URNs based on information that
   could be used to track individuals or compromise privacy.

Interoperability:

   The "mplify" namespace coexists with the "mef" namespace
   registered in RFC 7818 [RFC7818].  URNs previously assigned
   under "urn:mef:*" remain valid and are unaffected by this
   registration.

   Mplify Alliance will maintain clear documentation in the MANN
   registry (https://www.mplify.net/MANN/) to distinguish resources
   identified under the "mplify" namespace from those under the
   legacy "mef" namespace, avoiding ambiguity.

   Where a resource was originally identified by a "urn:mef:*" URN,
   that URN will not be reassigned or duplicated under "urn:mplify:*"
   unless explicitly documented as an equivalence mapping in the
   MANN registry.

   The hierarchical NSS structure is designed to avoid conflicts with
   other URN namespaces, URI schemes, and non-URN identifier systems.

Resolution:

   The "mplify" namespace is not currently registered with a
   Resolution Discovery System (RDS).  No resolution service is
   defined for this namespace at this time.

   Mplify Alliance publishes information regarding the resources
   identified by "urn:mplify:*" URNs in the MANN registry
   (https://www.mplify.net/MANN/).

Documentation:

   Mplify Alliance publishes specifications and supporting documents
   at https://www.mplify.net/.

   The existing "mef" URN namespace is documented in:

      RFC 7818 - URN Namespace for MEF Documents [RFC7818]

   This registration request serves as the initial documentation for
   the "mplify" URN namespace.  Mplify Alliance intends to publish
   a companion specification documenting the detailed policies and
   procedures governing URN assignment within this namespace.

Additional Information:

   Mplify Alliance (formerly MEF Forum) rebranded in June 2025.

   The "mef" URN namespace (RFC 7818) was registered when the
   organization was known as MEF Forum.  The "mplify" namespace
   is registered to reflect the new organizational identity while
   preserving the validity and persistence of all existing
   "urn:mef:*" assignments.

   The MANN registry (https://www.mplify.net/MANN/) serves as the
   authoritative source for all URN assignments under both the
   "mef" and "mplify" namespaces.

   For more information about Mplify Alliance activities, refer to
   https://www.mplify.net/.

Revision Information:

   This is the initial registration; no prior versions exist.
```
